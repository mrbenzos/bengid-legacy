1. Platform Purpose & Overview

The Bengid Legacy Ghana Limited platform is a bespoke multi-division e-commerce, automobile showroom, and general importation management system built specifically for the Ghanaian commercial environment. It empowers prospective buyers and enterprise clients across Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, and nationwide to discover authentic commercial printing supplies, inspect premium imported automobiles with transparent pricing or price-on-request options, place instant orders via WhatsApp, and request direct procurement quotes.

Key Client Advantage: The admin console requires zero technical or coding knowledge. Managers can add new printing supplies, list vehicles, update prices, enable the "Contact for Price" feature, mark items sold, and review customer inquiries through a clean visual dashboard on any desktop, laptop, or mobile smartphone.

4. Standard Operating Procedures (Daily Routine)

1
Morning Opening (8:30 AM)
Log into bengidlegacyghltd.com/admin using the secure OTP code sent to admin@bengidlegacy.com. Check the Leads tab for any inquiries submitted overnight and ensure the official WhatsApp phone (+233 20 576 1698) is charged, connected, and active.
2
Order & WhatsApp Inquiry Processing
When a customer clicks "Order via WhatsApp" or "Inquire on WhatsApp", review the pre-filled vehicle model or printing product name. Greet the customer professionally, confirm stock availability, provide custom quantity discounts if applicable, and arrange delivery or showroom viewing.
3
Stock & Showroom Updates
Whenever a new shipment arrives, an item runs low, or a vehicle is sold:
• If a vehicle is sold, open Cars in the admin panel and change status to Sold immediately.
• If paper or ink prices fluctuate, tick "Contact for Price" to direct buyers to direct WhatsApp quotation.
